# M2.1 — Frozen Reporting Contract

## Purpose

M2 is the publication/reporting layer for RankLab.

It may transform the frozen M1 outputs into tables, figures, README text,
reproducibility instructions, and paper-style narrative. It may not reopen
model fitting, evaluation, statistical inference, model selection, or support
definitions.

## Frozen headline

> **Winner identity is stable, but comparative margins are not.**

## Primary claims M2 may state

1. Popularity is the decisive winner in all four primary regime-target cells.
2. BPR and LightGCN are not decisively separated in any primary cell.
3. Popularity's measured advantage is substantially larger under randomized
   exposure than under standard logging.
4. Target definition changes Popularity's measured margin, especially under
   randomized exposure.

## Sensitivity claims M2 may state

1. The pre-specified shared-tabs sensitivity preserves the qualitative primary
   pattern.
2. The tab-1 sensitivity also preserves the qualitative pattern, but it remains
   descriptive only.

## Required limitations

Every substantial public-facing narrative should preserve the following
boundaries:

- Offline logging-policy contrasts are not causal treatment-effect estimates.
- Popularity is deterministic while BPR and LightGCN use five frozen seeds.
- BPR and LightGCN are not decisively separated under the frozen primary
  simultaneous intervals.
- `tab1` must not be assigned an undocumented semantic UI meaning.
- Native and matched-user target contrasts differ, so target-specific eligible
  user composition matters.
- LightGCN tuning fixed regularization while BPR searched regularization.

## Forbidden reporting moves

M2 must not:

- rerun scoring, tuning, bootstrapping, or model selection;
- invent a practical-significance threshold;
- promote sensitivity analyses into the primary multiplicity family;
- describe logging-policy contrasts as causal effects;
- claim BPR or LightGCN superiority over the other;
- attach semantic meaning to tab ID 1.
