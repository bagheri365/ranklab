# M1.4 — Primary Results Consolidation

## Purpose

M1.4 is a rendering/consolidation milestone only.

It performs no new model scoring, hyperparameter selection, bootstrap
resampling, or inferential decision. It reads the already-computed M1.1-M1.3
artifacts, verifies their immutable SHA-256 digests, and writes normalized
tables suitable for later reporting.

## Interpretation boundary

The consolidation may repeat decisions already produced by M1.2, including the
frozen decisive-winner classification.

For `G_AB` and `T_AB`, M1.4 carries forward the frozen effect-size point
estimates and 95% percentile intervals. It does not create an additional
significance-testing family or post-hoc practical-effect threshold.

## Primary statement structure

The generated Markdown explicitly separates:

1. decisive winner identity across the four primary cells;
2. BPR-versus-LightGCN simultaneous-interval separation;
3. cross-regime and cross-target margin effect sizes.

This keeps "winner stability" distinct from "margin stability."
