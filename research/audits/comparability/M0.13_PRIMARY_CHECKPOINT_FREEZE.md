## 10. Decision

The ten SHA256-identified primary checkpoints listed in this document are the fixed BPR and LightGCN model artifacts for subsequent RankLab evaluation.

They must not be replaced because later evaluation results are inconvenient.

Any replacement would require an explicit protocol amendment before M1 and a documented reason unrelated to evaluation performance.
