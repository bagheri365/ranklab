# M0.6b — Training contract decision

Status: **training subsection frozen; overall M0 protocol remains UNFROZEN**.

The primary training contract is now fixed from standard-policy history only:

- training dates: 2022-04-09 through 2022-04-16
- validation dates: 2022-04-17 through 2022-04-21
- pair unit: unique `(user_id, video_id)`
- positive: any click for the pair in the training slice
- eligible negative: exposed pair with no click anywhere in the training slice
- BPR-style sampling: one same-user logged negative per positive, uniformly with replacement, resampled each epoch using the model seed
- LightGCN graph: positive click pairs only
- validation ranking unit: one user
- validation candidate set: unique logged pairs in the validation window
- validation relevance: binary any-click
- validation objective: macro NDCG@10 using the frozen `<K` semantics once the evaluation section is finalized

The 5-day validation window was chosen from the prespecified 3/4/5-day candidates using only ranking depth, overlap, and sample-size adequacy. No Apr 22–May 8 labels or downstream model performance were used.

Model A is fixed as a deterministic clicked-pair popularity baseline: score each video by the number of distinct users with a positive click pair in the frozen training slice.

This decision does **not** freeze the overall M0 protocol. Evaluation candidate construction, matched-support policy, uncertainty procedures, and the final M1 protocol gate remain unresolved.
