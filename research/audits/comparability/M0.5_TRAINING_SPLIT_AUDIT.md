# M0.5 — Training interaction and temporal validation audit

## Purpose

M0.5 examines two implementation-critical choices **without freezing them**:

1. what standard-history interaction can train all three primary systems once while remaining distinct from the two M1 evaluation targets (`is_click`, `long_view`), and
2. whether the Apr 8–21 standard-history period supports a clean earlier-train / later-validation split.

## Candidate interaction definitions

The audit reports, but does not promote, three deliberately different candidates:

- **all logged exposures** — every standard-policy exposure is a positive interaction. This is dense and target-independent, but should be interpreted as learning historical exposure structure rather than direct preference.
- **any positive social action** — positive when any of `is_like`, `is_follow`, `is_comment`, or `is_forward` equals 1. This is behaviorally meaningful and distinct from both primary evaluation targets, but may be sparse and remains policy-conditioned.
- **downstream engagement** — positive when `is_profile_enter` equals 1 or any positive social action occurs. This remains distinct from `is_click` and `long_view` while testing whether profile-entry behavior provides enough additional support to avoid the extreme sparsity of social-only supervision.

The audit also reports raw prevalence for the component fields plus `is_hate` so the eventual choice is evidence-based.

## Temporal split audit

The default diagnostic split uses the last three **observed** dates in the standard-history file as validation and all earlier observed dates as training. This is a feasibility check only; M0 must still freeze the exact cutoff before M1.

The audit reports train/validation row counts, user/video support, candidate-positive density, and validation user/video coverage by the training portion.

## Guardrails

- Do not use `is_click` or `long_view` as the primary training supervision.
- Do not inspect Apr 22–May 8 comparative model outcomes to choose the interaction definition or validation cutoff.
- Do not freeze negative sampling, validation objective, or model-specific losses until the interaction definition is selected.
- A sparse but behaviorally meaningful signal is not automatically preferable to a dense exposure signal; interpretation and statistical support both matter.
- The downstream-engagement composite is a diagnostic candidate only; it must not be promoted merely because it is denser than social-only supervision.
