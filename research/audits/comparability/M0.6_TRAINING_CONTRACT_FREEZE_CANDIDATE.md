# M0.6 — Training Contract Freeze Candidate

This audit tests the proposed primary training contract before it is promoted into the frozen M0 protocol.

## Proposed contract

- source: standard-policy history only
- implicit positive: a user-video pair with at least one `is_click=1` exposure in the training slice
- logged negative: a user-video pair exposed in the training slice with no `is_click=1` exposure for that pair
- pair collapse happens before negative sampling, so a pair can never be both positive and negative
- BPR/LightGCN pairwise training samples one same-user logged negative per positive per epoch **uniformly with replacement**
- a pairwise-training user needs at least one positive pair and at least one eligible logged negative pair
- users with positives but zero logged negatives are excluded from pairwise training and explicitly reported
- LightGCN graph edges use positive click pairs only
- validation ranking unit: one validation user over that user's unique logged validation pairs
- validation relevance: binary any-click
- provisional tuning metric: macro NDCG@10 on eligible validation users

## Validation-window audit

Before freezing the split, compare exactly three trailing windows from the standard-history period:

- last 3 observed dates
- last 4 observed dates
- last 5 observed dates

Select the shortest window that provides adequate ranking depth, sample size, and train/validation user/item overlap. The choice must not use Apr 22–May 8 labels or downstream model performance.

## Guardrails

This audit must not inspect Apr 22–May 8 evaluation labels. `long_view` is not used for primary training or tuning. Same-user negatives may repeat across positive pairs within an epoch because sampling is with replacement; this is preferable to discarding users merely because they have fewer distinct negative pairs than positive pairs. The contract remains a freeze candidate until the real-data validation-window and retention statistics are reviewed.
