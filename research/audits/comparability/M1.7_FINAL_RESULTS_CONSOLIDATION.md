## Reporting structure

The final generated results document keeps three claims separate:

1. **Primary winner identity**
   - Popularity is the decisive winner in all four primary cells.
   - BPR and LightGCN are not decisively separated.

2. **Margin stability**
   - Popularity's pairwise advantage is substantially larger under randomized
     exposure than under standard logging.
   - Target definition also changes the Popularity margin, especially under
     randomized exposure.

3. **Support robustness**
   - The pre-specified shared-tabs sensitivity preserves the same qualitative
     pattern.
   - The tab-1 descriptive sensitivity also preserves the pattern, but it is
     not given semantic UI interpretation.

## Interpretation boundary

The final headline is:

> Winner identity is stable, but comparative margins are not.

This does not claim causal effects of logging policy. The logging-regime
contrasts are offline evaluation contrasts under different logged exposure
processes.
